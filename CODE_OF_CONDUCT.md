# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors, regardless of experience level, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, race, ethnicity, or religion.

## Expected Standards
- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other contributors.
- Respect differing perspectives, viewpoints, and feedback.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on what is best for the learning community and platform users.

## Scope & Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at `rohitam256@gmail.com`. All reports will be reviewed fairly and treated confidentially.
